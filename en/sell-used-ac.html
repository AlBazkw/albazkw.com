02 — Questions we hear

Do you buy old or used AC units?

Yes. We buy split, window, cassette, ducted, package and VRF units of any brand and any age, working or not. We pay cash or take the value off a new unit.

How do I get a price for my old AC?

Photograph the unit and the nameplate on it, which shows the brand, model and size, and send both with your area and the number of units. We reply with a price the same day.

Do you buy air conditioners that no longer work?

Yes. We buy faulty units too. The price depends on the type of unit, its size, age and condition.

Do you buy in bulk from buildings and offices?

Yes. We buy bulk lots from buildings, offices and renovation sites, and handle the uninstalling and removal.

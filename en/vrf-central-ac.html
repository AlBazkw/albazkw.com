02 — Questions we hear

What is the difference between a split AC and central air conditioning?

A split AC cools one room with its own indoor and outdoor unit. Central air conditioning cools several rooms from one unit through ducts, and a VRF system runs many indoor units from one outdoor unit with independent control per room. We install and maintain all three.

What is a VRF system?

VRF stands for variable refrigerant flow. One outdoor unit is connected to many indoor units and sends each room only the cooling it needs. Every room has its own temperature control.

When is VRF the better choice, and when is ducted central AC?

VRF suits buildings with many rooms used at different times, such as villas and offices, because each room runs independently. Ducted central AC suits large open spaces such as showrooms and halls. We recommend one after a load calculation and a look at the building.

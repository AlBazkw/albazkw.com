01 — Who we are

ALBAZ Engineering is an air conditioning company in Kuwait. We sell and install new units, replace and buy old ones, and maintain them all, for homes, offices, showrooms, schools and clinics in every Kuwait governorate.

We work on split, window, cassette, ducted, package and VRF systems, and stock the spare parts they need.

02 — How we work

Right-sized

We calculate the cooling load and recommend the unit the room needs, not the biggest one on the shelf.

Done properly

Vacuumed, leak-tested, charged by weight and commissioned. No shortcuts on the parts you cannot see.

Straight answers

A diagnosis before any price, an itemised quote, and an honest call between repair and replacement.

Old units have value

We buy your old unit instead of leaving you to dump it, and take its value off the new one.

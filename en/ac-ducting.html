02 — Questions we hear

Why does duct insulation matter?

Ducts usually run through very hot ceilings and roofs. Without good insulation and sealing the air warms up before it reaches the room and water condenses on the duct, so cooling drops and electricity use rises.

What is the difference between sheet metal duct and pre-insulated panels?

Galvanised sheet duct is made from metal sheet and then insulated. It is tough and suits long runs and large sizes. Pre-insulated panels are lighter and the insulation is part of the panel, so they install faster.

When does ductwork need cleaning?

When dust comes out of the air outlets, when there is a smell on start-up, when airflow weakens, and after renovation or painting work.

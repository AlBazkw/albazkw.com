03 — ALBAZ Engineering

تكييف في الكويت

ALBAZ Engineering is an air conditioning and HVAC company in Kuwait. We sell and install new split AC, window, cassette and ducted units, replace and upgrade old air conditioners, buy used AC units, and design central air conditioning and VRF systems for villas, offices and showrooms.

After the install: annual AC maintenance contracts, AC repair, ducting and insulation and spare parts, across Kuwait City, Hawalli, Farwaniya, Ahmadi, Jahra and Mubarak Al-Kabeer.

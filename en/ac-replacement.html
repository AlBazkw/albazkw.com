02 — Questions we hear

Should I repair or replace my old air conditioner?

If the unit is fairly new and the fault is a capacitor, fan motor or gas leak, repair is usually worth it. If it is old, needs a compressor, or runs on R22, replacing it with a new inverter unit is usually cheaper over time.

What is the benefit of upgrading to an inverter AC?

An inverter compressor changes speed to match what the room needs instead of switching on and off, so it uses less electricity, holds a steadier temperature and runs quieter.

Can the existing pipes be reused with a new AC?

Yes, when they are sound and suit the size and refrigerant of the new unit. We check them first and replace them where there is a leak or corrosion.

What happens to the old air conditioner?

We uninstall it, recover the refrigerant and take it away, and its value comes off the price of the new unit.

02 — Questions we hear

How often should an air conditioner be serviced in Kuwait?

With Kuwait’s dust and long cooling season, clean the filters monthly and have the unit serviced every three to four months, with a full check before summer. An annual maintenance contract schedules this for you.

What does an AC maintenance visit include?

Filter and coil cleaning, flushing the drain line, and checks on gas pressure, electrical connections and the thermostat.

When is the best time to service an AC?

In spring, before summer, so the unit is ready before the heat peaks. Then regular visits through the cooling season.

About us

Kuwait's air conditioning specialists

Al Baz Engineering was founded to give Kuwait a cooling contractor that sizes systems properly, installs them carefully and still answers the phone in August.

Our story

Built around one trade, done well

Since 2010 we have grown from a small split-unit installer into a full air conditioning company that sells new units, replaces and buys back old ones, and services them all, for homes, offices, showrooms, schools and clinics across Kuwait.

Our technicians are directly employed and trained on the brands we supply, so quality is controlled at every step rather than left to subcontractors. We stock our own spare parts, run our own maintenance schedules and stand behind every installation with a workmanship warranty.

  • Authorised supplier of new Gree, Midea, LG, Samsung, York and Carrier units
  • We buy old units of any brand and offer trade-in credit on new ones
  • Split, window, cassette, ducted, package and VRF systems
  • Own fleet of service vans with parts and refrigerant on board
  • Long-running maintenance contracts with repeat commercial clients

About us

Kuwaiti engineering, international standards

Al Baz Engineering was founded to give clients in Kuwait a contractor that engineers properly, builds safely and communicates clearly.

Our story

Built on engineering discipline

Since 2010 we have grown from a specialist MEP contractor into a multidisciplinary engineering company serving commercial, industrial and government clients across Kuwait.

Our engineers, supervisors and technicians are directly employed, trained and certified, so quality is controlled at every step rather than outsourced. We work with approved international manufacturers and follow Kuwait MEW, KFD and municipality requirements as a baseline, not a ceiling.

  • Classified contractor with the relevant Kuwait authorities
  • Licensed engineers across mechanical, electrical and civil disciplines
  • Documented QA/QC and HSE systems on every site
  • Long-term maintenance relationships with repeat clients

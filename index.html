How we work

From site survey to a cool room

We measure the space, check the electrical supply and calculate the cooling load before recommending anything.

Quote

An itemised proposal with the unit model, piping, ducting, labour and warranty, minus the trade-in value of your old unit.

Air conditioning company in Kuwait

New AC units, old unit replacement and buy-back, central air conditioning and HVAC maintenance across Kuwait

Al Baz Engineering is an air conditioning and HVAC contractor based in Kuwait. We sell and install new split AC, window, cassette and ducted units, replace and upgrade old air conditioners, buy used AC units for cash or trade-in credit, design central air conditioning and VRF systems for villas, offices and showrooms, and fabricate the ducting and insulation that makes central AC work in Kuwait's heat.

Once the system is in, our annual AC maintenance contracts keep every unit clean and efficient, our AC repair team answers emergency calls 24/7, and our parts store stocks compressors, motors, control boards and refrigerant for fast turnaround.

Air conditioning company in Kuwait

AC installation, central air conditioning and HVAC maintenance across Kuwait

Al Baz Engineering is an air conditioning and HVAC contractor based in Kuwait. We supply and install split AC, window, cassette and ducted units, design central air conditioning and VRF systems for villas, offices and showrooms, and fabricate the ducting and insulation that makes central AC work in Kuwait's heat.

Once the system is in, our annual AC maintenance contracts keep every unit clean and efficient, our AC repair team answers emergency calls 24/7, and our parts store stocks compressors, motors, control boards and refrigerant for fast turnaround.
